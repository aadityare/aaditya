interesting news in cyber

april 4th

north korean hackers spent 6 months posing as a trading firm

attending conferences across multiple countries, depositing $1M of real money, and sitting through actual strategy sessions with Drift contributors. once enough trust had been built, they took advantage of that trust and compromised admins (malicious repos, fake apps, a VSCode vuln, etc…) to extract $285M in 12 minutes. yes, big crypto theft is bad, but to me the more interesting part was the 6 month IRL infiltration.

april 9th

the FBI managed to recover deleted Signal messages

from a suspect's iPhone. not because of any vulnerability in Signal itself or by giving them a backdoor but rather by looking at the cache for push notifications which had message previews.

april 10th

a single threat actor breached nine Mexican government organizations

between Dec 2025 and Feb 2026, exfiltrating hundreds of millions of records by heavily leaning on Claude Code and GPT-4.1 as core tools. Claude handled recon, exploit customization, privilege escalation, and tunnel building while a 17k line Python script piped data to GPT-4.1 to generate intelligence reports across 305 internal servers. the LLMs did push back at some points but the attacker would consistently find ways to get around the "safety rails". the real takeaway isn't the vulnerabilities exploited but that one operator was moving at the speed of an entire red team.
